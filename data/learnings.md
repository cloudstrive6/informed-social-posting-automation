## Long-form (priority order)
1. **Lane C first (signal, n=5, median 47).** When a named next-gen drug gets a new trial result or a regulatory step, publish the long-form the same day, drug name first in the title, plus a 20:00 funnel Short. Score the 10-25 petrelintide long-form against this lane.
2. **Test "What Is [new drug]?" explainers between news cycles (hypothesis).** One a week on an under-covered pipeline drug with published human data: amycretin, MariTide, pemvidutide, mazdutide, petrelintide. Generic name first, dated status, no "beat" frame.
3. **Lane A: copy the Strength Training model, not the How Long model.** Practical tirzepatide "how to get the most from it" topics still gain views at day 9 (48). Timeline questions plateaued at 26. Food (11) and cost (3) lag. Keep 2 a week and score each at day 7 and day 28.
4. **Pause cost, coverage and Medicare long-forms (anecdote):** the Medicare $50 long-form has 3 views and its Short has 34. Every cost or coverage carousel reached 0–1.
5. **Pause Lane B gray-market long-forms (n=1, 6 views).** Gray-market works as a Short only. Allow one more test only with a fresh dated FDA, MHRA or Lilly event.
6. **No single-trial deep dives on approved drugs (median 8, n=5).** A topic that worked as a Short (SURMOUNT-5: 163 as a Short, 8 as a long-form) doesn't carry over.
7. **Novelty drives views, not the "beat" frame or title formula.** EloraTZP's title broke the current title rules (2 numbers, "T2D") and still won. Keep the rules (60 characters or fewer, at most 1 number, no abbreviations) for clarity, but they are unvalidated: no CTR data yet. Thumbnails: no signal, because layout is confounded with topic.
