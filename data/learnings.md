## Long-form: what earns minutes
- **Lane A is the default on every non-news day.** Make it one question a user would type, drug name first, title of 50 characters or fewer, at most 1 number.
- **Suggested video drives Lane A (proven).** RELATED_VIDEO gives about 70% of Lane A minutes (790 of 1,136), at 5–8 min per view: Strength 49 views/308 min, What to Eat 46/245, Side Effects 14/105, How Long 17/101. Every new Lane A video feeds the others, so keep the cluster tight.
- **Suggested traffic takes about 1 week to arrive.** Plateau (day 3: 70 impressions, AVD 85%) and Bones and Muscle (day 2: 55 impressions, all RELATED, AVD 79%) hold viewers very well on tiny samples. Judge them at day 7 (11-03/11-04), not before.
- **The first spoken sentence must answer the literal title.** Search minutes per view: What to Eat 2.2, How Long 2.1, Side Effects 1.3, versus Strength 0.24 (opens on lean-mass stats, not "where to start").
- **Playlist and subscriber viewers watch the longest** (Strength playlist 8 min per view; Side Effects subscribers 4.6 min per view). The PLAYLIST source now shows on Strength and orforglipron, so put every Lane A video in "Life on Tirzepatide".
- **Lane C is only for a fresh randomized result (within 48 h) on a drug people search for.** Status, filing and food-effect news goes into a Short.
- **Discovery-dead lanes stay paused (signal, n=6).** None drew more than 160 impressions: Medicare 19 min, pharmacy 31, petrelintide 56, elecoglipron 32, SURMOUNT-5 deep dive 32, HFpEF 35. Broad head-term titles are paused too.
- **Thumbnails (signal, still confounded by impression band):**
  - At 400+ impressions, before-after leads: How Long 5.2% (425), Side Effects 3.9% (718).
  - Reaction is weakest: Strength 2.6% (1,028), pill vs shot 2.6% (458).
  - Big-number is mixed: What to Eat 2.7% (1,150), orforglipron 4.5% (426).
  - Default to before-after, 4 words or fewer, one labelled character.
- **Minutes per impression:** How Long 0.48, orforglipron 0.47, survodutide 0.46, Strength 0.36, What to Eat 0.29, Side Effects 0.26 (lagged).
- **Packaging problem, not discovery:** What to Eat (1,150 impressions, 2.7%) and Strength (1,028, 2.6%) get the most impressions and the lowest CTR. Test a before-after thumbnail first, then retitle Strength to match its opening.
- **Retention:** Lane A AVD is 5.5–6.4 min (46–50%). Open on a "you" scenario that answers the title within 15 s. Re-hook every 45–75 s. Ask for the subscribe once, after the payoff.
- **Ending:** "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description." Never speak a title, and never say "on screen".

## Slots and production
- **The slot effect is small; topic decides.** Shorts at 08:00 / 13:00 / 20:00 ET, long-form at 14:00 ET.
- **Shorts: 60–85 s, 170 words or fewer.** Length (57–111 s) doesn't predict views.
- **Carousels reach 0–4 (proven, n≈50).** Stop them.
- **Masking:** mix at 0.02 or lower the first time, and measure last.
