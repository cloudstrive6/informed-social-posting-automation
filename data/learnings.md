## Long-form: what earns minutes
- **Suggested video carries Lane A (proven).** RELATED_VIDEO gives about 72% of mature Lane A minutes at 5–6 min a view. Search gives 0.2–2.5 min a view, and subscriber views 0.4–2.4.
- **The Shorts → long funnel is still zero (proven, 22 long-forms with sources).** None shows SHORTS or END_SCREEN traffic. PLAYLIST is too small to judge (6 views).
- **The first spoken sentence answers the literal title.** After it, open on a "you" scenario. Re-hook every 45–75 s, make one subscribe ask after the payoff, and end with "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description." Never speak a title.
- **Retention:** Lane A AVD is 5–6.4 min (38–50%). Bones & Muscle holds 73%, Food Noise about 90% (n=4 views), Plateau 43%.
- **Thumbnails at 400+ impressions (signal):**
  - Before-after (state change) leads: How Long 4.63%, Side Effects 3.87%, Protein 3.86%.
  - Big-number is mixed: orforglipron 4.01%, What to Eat 2.46%.
  - Reaction is weak: pill vs shot 2.57%, Strength 2.13% (still falling).
  - Default to a state change: 4 words or fewer, one labelled character, never a body before/after.
- **Packaging problem, not a discovery problem:** What to Eat (1,587 impressions, 2.46%) and Strength (1,593, 2.13%). Run Exp E on What to Eat only and leave everything else on it alone for 14 days. Strength is next, and it's now the worst CTR of any video at 1,000+ impressions.
- **Banned in long-form:** cost, Medicare, pharmacy and gray-market topics, "What is X?" explainers at 14:00, single-trial deep dives, broad head-term titles.
