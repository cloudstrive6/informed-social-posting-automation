## GLP-1 Shorts: what works on YouTube (n=18)
- **Proven: a named drug people recognise, plus one plain question, action or number.**
  - Top 7: bootleg "Reta" 128, tirzepatide muscle plan 122, Mounjaro metabolism 115, tirzepatide fat or muscle 98, anesthesia 82, survodutide 64, online retatrutide 57.
  - Their median is 98, about 1.7× the GLP-1 median.
- **Signal, getting stronger: tirzepatide or Mounjaro in the title.**
  - With tirzepatide/Mounjaro: median 98 (n=5: 122, 115, 98, 82, 56).
  - Semaglutide alone: median 32 (n=4). The pill is the best of that group at 53.
- **Signal, getting stronger: muscle on tirzepatide is our best evergreen lane.** Fat or muscle got 98; "How to Protect Your Muscle" got 122 in 2 days.
  - The winning hook: a direct "you" line, a named drug and a numbered promise ("Losing weight on tirzepatide? 3 ways to protect your muscle"), answered with data by about 15 s.
- **Signal: warnings about gray-market or "research" products work** (n=3: bootleg Reta 128, online retatrutide 57, plague "cures online" 82).
  - Keep the tone calm and attributed: FDA reports and the manufacturer's statements, plus "what's confirmed vs not".
  - Never name a seller or say how to buy. Always point to a prescriber.
- **Signal: a "what's confirmed?" news decoder works when a named drug is in the news** (Reta 128, plague Russia 88). News fades within days, so don't build a lane on one event.
- **Signal: scare topics about approved drugs land low**, even when the script is calm (pancreatic cancer 25, NAION 32). The difference from the Reta Short: that one was a warning about unapproved products, not a scare about an approved drug.
- **Signal: "stopping a GLP-1" is weak on YouTube** (n=2: pilot trial 25, continuity 2). Both titles had no named drug. If we try again, put a named drug and trial in the title (for example tirzepatide, SURMOUNT-4).
- **Underperformers:**
  - generic or unnamed comparators ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug", 2; "a small pilot trial", 25);
  - stigma or feelings questions ("cheating", 6);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 24, ACHIEVE-4 33);
  - teasing a number without giving it (HRT, 5).
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first.
  - At most one number, with its hedge.
  - At most one abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
