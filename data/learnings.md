## Long-form craft
- **Shorts → long funnel: still zero (proven, 23 long-forms).** SHORTS and END_SCREEN traffic is 0, and PLAYLIST has about 9 views in total.
  - **The cheapest unused lever is EloraTZP itself, our highest-traffic page.** Give it an end screen and a line-1 ▶ Watch next link pointing to Side Effects and Stopping.
- **Retention (d7 curves, n=8 long-forms):**
  - The median hold at 30 s is about 72%.
  - In 6 of 8 videos the biggest drop comes at 15–25 s, about 3% into the video. That's right after the first answer, where the open loop or roadmap begins:
    - Strength: −36 points at 21 s
    - What to Eat: −16
    - Survodutide: −17
    - Orforglipron: −14
    - EloraTZP: −12.5
    - Plague 101: −40
  - **Fix to test:** at 20–30 s, give a second concrete payoff (one number or the first practical step), keep the open loop to one sentence, and never use a roadmap or channel intro there.
- **Openings that work (signal):**
  - "You" scenario openings hold best at 30 s: What to Eat 81%, How Long 80%.
  - A direct question with the answer first holds well: retatrutide 78%, EloraTZP 77%.
  - A counterintuitive claim that doesn't match the title holds worst: Strength 41%, where "lean mass" opened a "Where to Start" title. Deliver the title's literal promise within 20 s.
- **The midpoint is weak.** `relative_pct_mid` is 19–54 on all 8 videos, below YouTube's typical 50 for most of them. Re-hook every 45–75 s, with a payoff before each chapter change.
- **Length (signal, n=6):** cuts of about 10 min get the best AVD (Bones 63%, Fatigue 62%, Constipation 54%, Stopping 49%). Aim for 9–11 min.
- **Titles (signal, test it):** all four of the top Lane A videos carry "(Mounjaro)" or "(Mounjaro/Zepbound)", and "mounjaro" and "zepbound" are our top Lane A search terms. Later titles without the brand get less search (Stopping 12 search views). Use "Tirzepatide (Mounjaro) …": generic name first, brand in brackets for recognition only.
- **Thumbnails (signal, n=4 at 1,000+ impressions):**
  - State change: Side Effects 4.18%, Protein 3.63%.
  - Big number: What to Eat 2.81%.
  - Reaction: Strength 2.13%.
  - Below about 300 impressions, CTR doesn't separate layouts. Default to a state change with 4 words or fewer.
- **Exp E** (What to Eat and Strength swaps): read it on 11-24 and change nothing before then.
- **Still banned in long-form:**
  - cost, Medicare, pharmacy and gray-market topics
  - evergreen "What is X?" or "Did X beat tirzepatide?" videos on pipeline drugs
  - single-trial deep dives
