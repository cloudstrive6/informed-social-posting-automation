- **Proven: a named drug people recognise, plus one plain question or one number.**

- **Signal: tirzepatide or Mounjaro in the title beats semaglutide alone.**
  - Tirzepatide/Mounjaro: median 90 (n=4).
  - Semaglutide alone: median 32 (n=4). The semaglutide pill (46) is the best of that group.

- **Signal: myth-busts about online or "unproven" products work.** Online "retatrutide" 56. The plague "cures online" Short got 82.
  - Warn about gray-market sources. Never name a seller or say how to buy.
- **Underperformers:**
  - generic comparators ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug", 2);
  - stigma or feelings questions ("cheating", 6);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 23, ACHIEVE-4 33);
  - teasing a number without giving it (HRT, 5).
- **Title rules:**
  - At most one number, with its hedge.
  - At most one abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
