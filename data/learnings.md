## Long-form retention (d7; compare long with long only)
- **Hold at 30 s:** median ≈77% (n=24). The biggest drop falls at 12–35 s in 17 of 24 curves (proven). Exp H openers: median ≈80% at 30 s (signal, n=4).
- **Never open with "Short answer." or a list** (First Month lost 23 points at 14 s).
- **Never use a standalone "Picture this" scene** (Thanksgiving −25 at 13 s, Diarrhea −12.5 at 14 s).
- **No trial-setup scenes (n=5).** Examples: "Let's meet the trial" (Stopping −11.7 at 70 s) and the enrolment count (Constipation −25 at 75 s). Give the result first and put the population on screen only.
- **Endings:** land the last new fact before 80% (Months 2–6 −8.4 at 81%). Lists have 3 items at most (Plateau −16.7 at item three). Never end a chapter on a warning card (Alcohol −9.1 at 70%).
- **The midpoint is weak** (median relative retention ≈44). Re-hook every 45–75 s and keep videos to 9–11 min.

  - No trial name as the first words of scene 2.
  - Lists have 2 items. One rounded number per sentence.
- **Hook archetypes:** topic confounds them, so none is proven.
