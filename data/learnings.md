## GLP-1 Shorts: what works (n=14)
- **Proven: a named drug people recognise, plus one plain question or one number.**
  - Top 6: Mounjaro metabolism 115, tirzepatide fat or muscle 98, surgery/anesthesia 82, survodutide 63, CagriSema vs tirzepatide 56, online "retatrutide" 55.
  - Their median is about 70, roughly 1.8× the GLP-1 median.
- **Signal: "what does it do to my body?" questions lead.** Metabolism, fat vs muscle and surgery are our top 3.
- **Signal: safety-scare topics land below the median, even when calm and hedged.** Pancreatic cancer 23, vision loss (NAION) 32, food noise 32 (n=3).
- **Signal: tirzepatide or Mounjaro in the title beats semaglutide alone.** Median 90 (n=4) vs 32 (n=3). Small sample, so don't over-fit.
- **Signal: a practical "you" action works.** "Tell your anesthesia team" got 82.
- **Underperformers:**
  - generic comparators ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug", 2);
  - stigma or feelings questions ("cheating", 6);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 23, ACHIEVE-4 33);
  - teasing a number without giving it (HRT, 5).
- **Title rules:**
  - At most one number, with its hedge.
  - At most one abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
- **Signal: myth-busts about online or "unproven" products work.** Online "retatrutide" 55. Plague "cures" online 82 on YouTube and 23 reach on Instagram.
  - The GLP-1 version warns about gray-market sources. It never names a seller and never says how to buy.
