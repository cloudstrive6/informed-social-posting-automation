## Long-form retention (d7; compare long with long only)
- **The median hold at 30 s is about 77% (n=23).** The biggest drop falls at 12–35 s in 17 of 23 curves (proven).
- **Exp H is at n=3:** Alcohol 94.5%, Keeping Results 77.7%, First Month 66.7%.
  - **Keeping Results lost 11.7 points at 25 s,** in the scene that opens "In SURMOUNT-4, the key tirzepatide trial on stopping, adults with obesity, or…".
  - Fix to test: speak the number first ("About 14% came back on placebo, and people stayed about 10% below their start"). Put the population on screen, not in the narration.
- **Never open with "Short answer." or a summary list.** First Month lost 23 points at 14 s.
- **Never use a standalone "Picture this" scene (signal, n=3).** Fold the "you" moment into sentence 1.
- **Answer-first or "you" openers hold 77–95% at 30 s.** News or dated-status openers hold 63–72% (signal, n≈11).
- **Trial-setup scenes lose viewers (n=4).** Examples: "Let's meet the trial", the enrolment count, and the SURMOUNT-4 population line. Give the result and the population in one sentence, with the result first.
- **Don't end a chapter on a warning card** (Alcohol −9.1 points at 70%).
- **Land the last new fact before 80% and keep lists to 3 items** (Plateau −16.7 at item three).
- **Re-hook every 45–75 s and keep videos to 9–11 min.** The midpoint is weak (median relative retention about 44).
