## YouTube Shorts (median about 58 views, n=44)
- **Retention predicts reach (proven).**
  - Shorts held at ≥70%: median about 103 views (n=8).
  - Loop endings went past 100% (retatrutide T2D 108%, heart failure 188%).
  - Exceptions exist: macular 104 views at 14%, anaesthesia 83 at 13%. A hot topic can reach people even when they swipe away.
- **Generic "tirzepatide" in the title (proven): median about 100 (n=15).** Brand-first titles (Mounjaro, Medicare Wegovy/Zepbound) get 33–37.
- **Body-worry "honest uncertainty" Shorts (signal, n=6):** bones 231, nausea 130, macular 105, body composition 102, grip 8.
- **Retatrutide gets the highest peaks (n=7):** 262, 199, 120. New Phase 3 data and "what's confirmed?" angles win. Status titles get 40.
- **Two-drug head-to-heads with tirzepatide named first (signal): median about 104 (n=5).** Use a different trial each time.
- **Gray-market "what's confirmed?" (signal): median about 105 (n=7).** At most 1 a week.
- **Muscle works as an outcome question (133, 102), not as a "3 Ways" list (32).**
- **Semaglutide-only titles underperform (median about 33).** Exception: body-worry topics where the evidence is semaglutide.
- **Banned:** "Is X approved yet?" titles, observational surgery angles (5 views), cost or coverage Shorts, scare framing.
