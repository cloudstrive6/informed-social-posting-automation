- **Underperformers:**
  - generic or unnamed comparators (2, 25);
  - stigma questions (6);
  - scare topics about approved drugs (pancreatic cancer 25, NAION 32);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 25, ACHIEVE-4 33);
  - "stopping" titles with no drug named (25, 2);
  - "not approved yet" product status (patch 17);
  - titles that tease a number without giving it (5).
- **Crowded titles hold even tirzepatide back.** HFpEF with 3 abbreviations and 2 numbers got 59.
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first, brand in brackets.
  - At most 1 number, with its hedge.
  - At most 1 abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
