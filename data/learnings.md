## Long-form: what earns watch minutes (n=15 GLP-1 with analytics)
- **Medians:** about 29 views and about 51 watch minutes per video. The top 5 by minutes are EloraTZP 1,474, Orforglipron 162, Strength Training 129, How Long 122 and Survodutide 119.
- **Lane A, practical tirzepatide (signal, n=4), is the most reliable earner.**
  - Minutes: 129, 122, 53, 39 (median about 88). That's about 1.7× the channel median.
  - 35–53% viewed, AVD 263–400 s.
  - How Long earned the most per view: 3.0 min per viewCount.
- **Lane C, next-gen drugs (signal, n=8):** a view median of about 29, and about 51 min without EloraTZP.
  - The outlier was a fresh result (EASD, published within days) with a big head-to-head number against a drug people know.
  - The "Did X beat Y?" title alone doesn't do it: CagriSema 24, Retatrutide 29, HFpEF 17 (n=4 including EloraTZP, median about 27). Freshness and the size of the number did the work, not the title formula.
- **Bare "What Is [new drug]?" (anecdote):** petrelintide had 4 views at day 2, while its Short reached 105 on Instagram. Keep these explainers paused.
- **Paused (low on both views and minutes):**
  - Approved-drug single-trial deep dives (SURMOUNT-5 9, HFpEF 17, CagriSema 24).
  - Cost, Medicare and pharmacy topics (8 views each; 0 and 20 min).
  - Broad head-term titles: "Tirzepatide vs Semaglutide…" got 9 views while its Short got 163.
- **Thumbnail layout: no signal.** View medians: versus 17 (n=3, one being EloraTZP), reaction 29 (n=7), big-number 36 (n=3), before-after 33 (n=2). Topic is confounded with layout. Keep the packaging rules but don't pick a layout on this data.
- **Retention pattern (signal):**
  - Open on a concrete "you" scenario and give the first answer within 15 s (How Long 53%, Side Effects 50%).
  - Openings built on trial numbers or long setup do worse (TRIUMPH-2 32%, Retatrutide 29%; Plague Today 2%).
  - At 11–14 min we get about 4–7 min watched. Cut setup; don't pad.
- **Test (hypothesis):** "Tirzepatide and Your Bones and Muscle". The bones Short reached 235 and the muscle Short 133. Score the long-form on watch minutes at day 7.

## YouTube Shorts (median about 60 views, n≈45 GLP-1)
- **Retention predicts reach (proven).** GLP-1 Shorts held at ≥70% have a median of about 93 views (n=8). Loop endings went past 100% (retatrutide T2D 108%/262 views, heart failure 188%).
  - Exceptions exist: hot topics reached people at under 20% viewed (macular 105, anaesthesia 83).
- **Generic "tirzepatide" in the title (proven): median about 100 (n≈15).** Brand-first titles get 33–37.
- **Body-worry "honest uncertainty" Shorts (signal, n=7): bones 235, nausea 147, macular 105, body composition 103, gallbladder 41 at day 1, grip 8.**
  - The bones Short reached 235 with "GLP-1 Meds" and no drug name in the title. The body-worry subject can carry a title on its own. The no-drug-name ban still applies to every other format.
- **Retatrutide gets the highest peaks (n=7): 262, 199, 120.** New Phase 3 data and "what's confirmed?" angles win. Status titles get 40.
- **Two-drug head-to-heads with tirzepatide named first (signal): median about 104 (n=5).** Use a different trial each time.
- **Gray-market "what's confirmed?" (signal): median about 105 (n=7).** At most 1 a week.
- **Muscle works as an outcome question (133, 103), not as a "3 Ways" list (35).**
- **Underperformers:**
  - Semaglutide-only titles: median about 33.
  - Observational surgery angle: 5.
  - Cost and Medicare: 37.
  - "Approved yet?" status titles: 40–65.

## Instagram Reels

- **Low reach on Instagram:** gray-market, recall, cost and status Reels (0–30).

## Slots and production
- **Slot effect is small; topic decides.** Shorts at 08:00 / 13:00 / 20:00 ET. Long-form at 14:00 ET.
- **Shorts length (57–111 s) doesn't predict views.** Aim for 60–85 s and 170 words or fewer.
- **Masking has cost at least 7 pieces.** Mix at 0.02 or lower the first time.
- **Carousels reach 0–4 (proven, n≈38 on Instagram).** Stop them, or reuse verified Short text only.
