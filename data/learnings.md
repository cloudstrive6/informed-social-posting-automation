## Long-form retention (d7, n=20; compare long with long only)
- **Median hold at 30 s is about 77%.** The biggest drop comes at 12–35 s in 15 of 20 curves (proven).
- **New (signal, n=3): the "you" scenario placed after the answer is where viewers leave.** Each drop was on the scene that opens the scenario:
  - Thanksgiving: 13 s, −25 points, "Picture it. The turkey's out…"
  - Diarrhea: 14 s, −12.5 points, "Picture this. You're three days past a dose increase…"
  - Fatigue: 22 s, −13.3 points, "You've barely eaten since breakfast…"
  - **Fix to test:** keep the scenario as half of sentence 1 ("You're three days past a dose increase on tirzepatide, and…"). After the short answer, go straight to the second concrete payoff (a number or the first step). Never start a new "Picture this" scene between 10 and 30 s.
- **"You" openers in sentence 1 still hold (signal, n≈10):** s30 of 77–92%. News or status openers hold 63–72%. Elecoglipron lost 30 points at 16 s on a dated "isn't approved" line.
- **Trial setup and caveat scenes cost viewers (signal, n=3).**

  - Open each chapter with the result and the population in one sentence. Fold caveats into the payoff line.
- **Late drop at 73–85% (n=3).** How Long dropped on a tip line and Plateau on "Three: fluids". Land the last new fact before about 80% and keep lists to 3 items.
- **The midpoint is weak (proven).** Median rel_mid is about 44; Diarrhea is at 26.4. Re-hook every 45–75 s and aim for 9–11 minutes.
