## Long-form retention (d7, n=22; compare long with long only)
- **The median hold at 30 s is about 77%.** The biggest drop falls at 12–35 s in 16 of 22 curves (proven).
- **Exp H (answer first, then a second number by 30 s) is mixed: n=2.**
  - Alcohol held 94.5% at 30 s.
  - First Month held 66.7%. Its biggest drop was −23.1 points at 14 s, in the scene opening "Short answer. Tirzepatide turns appetite down. Stomach side effects are common…" (6–15 s).
  - Fix to test: never open with a "Short answer." label or a summary list. Sentence 2 should be one concrete thing the viewer will feel that week, then the number. Keep Exp H and read How It Works, Keeping Results and Months 2–6 as their curves arrive.
- **A standalone "Picture this / you" scene after the answer loses viewers (signal, n=3):** Thanksgiving −25, Diarrhea −12.5, Fatigue −13.3. Fold the scenario into sentence 1.
- **Answer-first or "you" openers hold 77–95% at 30 s. News or dated-status openers hold 63–72% (signal, n≈11).** Example: Elecoglipron lost −30 points on "As of the twenty-sixth of October…".
- **Trial setup and caveat scenes lose viewers (signal, n=3):** "Let's meet the trial", the enrolment line, "a different drug". Give the result and the population in one sentence.
- **Don't end a chapter on a warning card** (Alcohol −9.1 points at 70%). Say the sign and the action in one line inside the payoff, then go straight to the next chapter's question.
- **Late drops come at 70–85% of the video (n=4).** Land the last new fact before 80%. Keep lists to 3 items. The midpoint is weak (median relative retention about 44): re-hook every 45–75 s and keep videos to 9–11 minutes.
