## Long-form retention (d7, n=22; compare long with long only)
- **The median hold at 30 s is about 77%.** The biggest drop falls at 12–35 s in 16 of 22 curves (proven).
- **Exp H is still n=2.** No d7 curve has arrived yet for How It Works (day 8) or Keeping Results (day 7). That means the data is late, not missing.
  - Alcohol held 94.5% at 30 s.
  - First Month held 66.7%. It lost 23.1 points at 14 s, in the scene that opens "Short answer. Tirzepatide turns appetite down…".
  - Rule: never open with a "Short answer." label or a summary list. Sentence 2 is one concrete thing the viewer will feel that week, then the number.
- **A standalone "Picture this / you" scene after the answer loses viewers (signal, n=3):** Thanksgiving −25, Diarrhea −12.5, Fatigue −13.3. Fold the scenario into sentence 1.
- **Opener type matters (signal, n≈11).** Answer-first or "you" openers hold 77–95% at 30 s. News or dated-status openers hold 63–72%. Example: Elecoglipron lost 30 points on "As of the twenty-sixth of October…".
- **Trial setup and caveat scenes lose viewers (signal, n=3).** Examples: "Let's meet the trial", the enrolment line, "a different drug". Give the result and the population in one sentence.
- **Don't end a chapter on a warning card** (Alcohol −9.1 points at 70%). Put the sign and the action in one line inside the payoff.
- **Late drops come at 70–85% of the video (n=4).** Land the last new fact before 80% and keep lists to 3 items.
  - Plateau lost 16.7 points at item "Three: fluids".
  - The midpoint is weak (median relative retention about 44). Re-hook every 45–75 s and keep videos to 9–11 minutes.

- **Keep the spoken lines lean (signal):**
  - Stacked spoken stats at 4–12 s lose viewers (n=6). Use one rounded number per sentence and put the comparator on screen.
