## NEW: impressions and CTR (n=20 long-forms)
- **Discovery is the main problem, not CTR (signal).**
  - 14 of 20 long-forms got 300 impressions or fewer.
  - The paused lanes had high CTR but almost no impressions: Medicare 45 impressions at 6.7%, pharmacy 55 at 5.5%, petrelintide 19, elecoglipron 26. YouTube doesn't surface these topics, so better packaging can't fix them. **Keep them paused.**
- **Packaging problems: plenty of impressions, CTR under 3%.**
  - Lane A: Strength (593 impressions, 2.9%) and What to Eat (484, 2.7%).
  - Off-niche: Plague 101 (1,046, 1.8%) and Madagascar (410, 2.2%).

- **Thumbnail layout by CTR (signal, confounded by topic and impression volume):**
  - reaction: median about 6.3% (n=7)
  - before-after: 6.5–7.3% (n=2)
  - versus: about 3.8% (n=4)
  - **big-number: median about 2.5% (n=6), the weakest layout.** Avoid big-number as the default on Lane A until it wins a test.
- **New metric: minutes per impression (higher is better).**
  - Side Effects 1.03, EloraTZP 0.79, Orforglipron 0.59, Survodutide 0.50, How Long 0.49, Strength 0.37, Medicare 0.16, What to Eat 0.11.
  - Lane A earns as many minutes per impression as hit news videos once the click happens, so getting more impressions for Lane A is the lever.

## Traffic sources (n=17 long-forms)

## Long-form: what earns minutes (GLP-1)
- **Lane A, practical tirzepatide (signal, n=4), is the default.**
  - Minutes: Strength 218, How Long 136, Side Effects 113 (at day 3), What to Eat 54. Median about 125, about 2.4× the channel.
  - They hold 39–57% viewed and keep earning after week 1.
- **Lane C, fresh randomized head-to-head on a drug people search for, is the only proven spike** (EloraTZP). Other next-gen videos earn a median of about 45 min. Orforglipron (174) worked because it had a news peg and a known drug.
- **Paused, with impressions confirming it:**
  - Lane P "What Is [drug]?"
  - Cost, Medicare and pharmacy topics
  - Approved-drug single-trial deep dives (SURMOUNT-5: 9 views, 129 impressions; HFpEF: 17 views)
  - Broad head-term titles
- **Title:** drug name first, the long-tail question a user would type, about 50 characters or fewer, 1 number at most.
- **Thumbnail:** 4 words or fewer; reaction or before-after with one labelled character. No big-number by default.
- **Retention:** open on a "you" scenario that answers the title within 15 s. Re-hook every 45–75 s, cut setup, one subscribe ask after the payoff.

## Slots and production
- **Slot effect is small; topic decides.** Shorts at 08:00 / 13:00 / 20:00 ET, long-form at 14:00 ET.
- **Shorts length (57–111 s) doesn't predict views.** Aim for 60–85 s and 170 words or fewer.
- **Carousels reach 0–4 (proven, n≈45).** Stop them, or reuse verified Short text only.
- **Masking has cost at least 7 pieces.** Mix at 0.02 or lower the first time.
