## Long-form craft

  - Links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable. Only the owner's related-video link works.

- **Opening and structure:** the first sentence answers the literal title, then a "you" scenario. Re-hook every 45–75 s. One subscribe ask, after the payoff. End with "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description."

- **Calm body-worry Shorts with tirzepatide in the title are still the best format (proven, n=18). Median about 93, roughly 1.5× the channel.**

- **Spikes come from the feed, not retention.** The hair Short has 23% viewed. Hair, injection site and nausea are demand signals. Retention above about 60% tracks with steadier views.
- **Title rules:** "Is this normal?" or "Will it…?" first, with tirzepatide first in the title. Semaglutide-only titles get about 40 views unless the evidence is semaglutide-only.
- **Head-to-heads:** with tirzepatide first in an obesity population they get 108–176. Diabetes-only ones get 31–60.
- **Retatrutide:** new Phase 3 data or a "what's confirmed?" angle works (T2D 280, bootleg 213, Reddit 126, confirmed 81). Knee OA got 95. Approval-status titles got 52.
- **Underperformers:** observational, survey or grocery angles (8–9; nails 9), surgery (8), cost and Medicare (42), "3 Ways" lists (38), drug names nobody searches for (tradipitant 39, petrelintide 29, ATTAIN-J 28), add-on-indication trials (psoriatic arthritis 28 by day 1).
