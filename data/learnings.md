  - Titles with "(Mounjaro)" or starting with "Mounjaro": median 107 (n=4).

- **Signal: the muscle lane works only as a question or outcome.** Three Shorts: 130, 99, 24. The 24 was "3 Ways to Start Strength Training on Tirzepatide", which put the drug last and used a how-to frame. It has settled low by day 2. Don't repeat that frame.

- **Underperformers:**
  - unnamed comparators (2, 25);
  - stigma (6);
  - scare topics about approved drugs (pancreatic cancer 25, NAION 32);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 26, ACHIEVE-4 34);
  - "stopping" titles with no drug named (25);
  - "not approved yet" product status (patch 18);
  - numbered how-to titles (24).
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first, brand in brackets.
  - At most 1 number, with its hedge.
  - At most 1 abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
  - Crowded titles hold even tirzepatide back (HFpEF got 65).
