## Long-form retention (d7 curves, n=18, long-form only)
- **The median hold at 30 s is about 77%.** Best: Constipation 91.7 (small n), Petrelintide 89.5, Plateau 83.3, Bones 82.8, What to Eat 81.4. Worst: Strength 41.4 (counterintuitive claim that didn't match the title) and Protein 51.8 (subscriber audience).
- **The biggest drop comes at 12–35 s in 13 of 18 curves (proven),** right after the first answer, where the open loop or roadmap starts.
  - **Fix:** at 15–30 s, give a second concrete payoff (one number or the first practical step), keep the open loop to one sentence, and never put a roadmap or channel intro there.
- **New (n=2): Stopping and Constipation both drop at 70–75 s (11% in), at the first chapter change.**
  - **Fix:** land a payoff before chapter 1 ends, and open chapter 2 with a question, not with background.
- **3 curves drop late, at 73–85%** (How Long, Plateau, Bones), where the recap or checklist starts.
  - **Fix:** land the last promised payoff before that block and start the end-screen ask at about 85%.
- **"You" scenario openers hold best (signal, n≈9):** s30 of 77–92%. News or stakes openers hold 63–72%.
- **The midpoint is weak (proven).** `relative_pct_mid` has a median of about 43 and is below 50 on 13 of 18 curves.
  - **Fix:** re-hook every 45–75 s. Stopping (51.9) and Strength (84) are the exceptions to copy.
- **Length:** cuts of about 10 min give the best AVD. Aim for 9–11 min.
