## Traffic sources: the new finding (n=16 long-forms)
- **Search finds viewers but doesn't keep them.** YT_SEARCH brought 768 of about 1,000 analytics views but only about 2.0 min per view, and about **1.3 min per view without EloraTZP**. Examples: Strength search gave 20 views and 5 min; Retatrutide 19 and 5; Plague 101 49 and 28.
- **Suggested (RELATED_VIDEO) viewers watch about 5× longer.** 105 views earned 673 min, about **6.4 min per view**. Examples: Strength 26 views/165 min, Orforglipron 12/97, How Long 7/57, Side Effects 5/46.
  - Suggested traffic goes mostly to practical, evergreen videos. YouTube puts them next to other GLP-1 videos.
- **Shorts → long-form funnel = zero (proven, n=17).** No long-form has a single SHORTS-source view. END_SCREEN = 0. PLAYLIST is tiny (EloraTZP 7, Strength 2, Orforglipron 1). Exp D (link in the description) is our only live funnel test.
- **Read for search bounces (hypothesis):** search viewers leave in under 20 s when the first 15 s don't answer the literal title query. "Strength Training… Where to Start" opened on a lean-mass stat, not on where to start.
  - Rule: the first spoken sentence answers the exact title question in plain words. Context comes after.

## Long-form: what earns watch minutes (GLP-1, n=17; median 30 views, about 52 min)
- **Lane A, practical tirzepatide (signal, n=4), is the reliable earner and keeps compounding.**
  - Minutes: Strength 218, How Long 136, Side Effects 113, What to Eat 54. Median about 125, **about 2.4× the channel median**.
  - They hold 39–57% viewed and earn about 3 min per viewCount.
  - Minutes keep coming after week 1 (Strength went 129 → 218 and Side Effects 53 → 113 in the latest pull).
- **Lane C, fresh next-gen results (signal, n=7):** the median without EloraTZP is about 45 min. Only a fresh randomized head-to-head against a known drug has spiked. Orforglipron (174 min) worked because it had a news peg and a drug people already search for.
- **Lane P, bare "What Is [drug]?" (anecdote, n=2): petrelintide 7 views/0 min, elecoglipron 7 views at day 2.** Keep it paused. Don't revive it without a news peg.
- **Keep paused:**
  - Cost, Medicare and pharmacy topics (8–10 views, 7–20 min).
  - Approved-drug single-trial deep dives (SURMOUNT-5 9, HFpEF 17).
  - Broad head-term titles.
- **Thumbnail layout: no signal yet.** Before-after has 2 wins (136, 113), but both are Lane A, so topic is confounded. Don't choose a layout on this data.
- **Retention:** open on a "you" scenario that answers the title within 15 s. Re-hook every 45–75 s. At 11–14 min we get 4–7 min watched, so cut setup.

## YouTube Shorts (median about 60 views, n≈50 GLP-1)
- **Retention predicts reach (proven):** Shorts held at ≥70% have a median of about 93 views. Loop endings went past 100% (retatrutide T2D 266 views at 107%, heart failure 188%).
- **Generic "tirzepatide" in the title (proven): median about 100.** Brand-first titles get 33–37. Semaglutide-only titles get a median of about 33.
- **Body-worry "honest uncertainty" Shorts (signal, n=8):** bones 235, nausea 149, macular 105, body composition 104, blood pressure 79 at day 2, gallbladder 43, grip 8. Bones reached people at 24% viewed, so the topic itself carries reach.
- **Retatrutide gets the highest peaks:** new Phase 3 data and "what's confirmed?" angles reached 266, 199 and 120. Status titles got 40–42.
- **Head-to-heads with tirzepatide named first (signal, n=5): median about 104.** Gray-market "what's confirmed?" (n=7): median about 105, at most 1 a week.
- **Underperformers:** "Approved yet?" titles (40–66), "3 Ways" lists (35), the observational surgery angle (5), cost and Medicare (37).

## Instagram Reels
- **Instagram doesn't track YouTube (proven).** Calm body and benefit questions win: body composition 238, macular 178, blood pressure 172, grip 164, nausea 120, petrelintide 105.
  - Bones got only 33 on Instagram against 235 on YouTube, so judge each platform separately.
- **Low reach on Instagram:** gray-market, recall, cost and status Reels (0–30).

## Slots and production
- **Slot effect is small; topic decides.** Shorts at 08:00 / 13:00 / 20:00 ET. Long-form at 14:00 ET.
- **Shorts length (57–111 s) doesn't predict views.** Aim for 60–85 s and 170 words or fewer.
- **Carousels reach 0–4 (proven, n≈40).** Stop them, or reuse verified Short text only.
- **Masking has cost at least 7 pieces.** Mix at 0.02 or lower the first time.
