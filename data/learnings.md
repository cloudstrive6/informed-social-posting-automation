## Long-form: what earns minutes
- **Lane A, practical tirzepatide, is the default.** Make it one question a user would type, drug name first, title of 50 characters or fewer, at most 1 number.
- **Suggested video (RELATED_VIDEO) drives Lane A (proven, n=7 videos).** It supplies 62% of Lane A minutes, at 5.5–9 min per view: Strength 34 views/218 min, What to Eat 17/93, How Long 12/77, Side Effects 5/46. Practical, evergreen videos get recommended next to each other, so build a tight linked cluster.
- **Search viewers bounce when the opening doesn't pay off the title.** Strength gets 0.25 min per search view ("Where to Start", but it opens on lean-mass stats). Retatrutide gets 0.26 ("Did it beat…?", answered "no trial yet"). Titles that pay off fast hold 1.8–2.5 min (How Long, What to Eat, EloraTZP). Rule: the first spoken sentence answers the literal title.
- **Playlist viewers watch the longest** (EloraTZP 7 views/63 min; Strength 2/16). Once the "Life on Tirzepatide" playlist exists, every Lane A video goes in it.
- **Lane C (a fresh randomized result on a drug people search for, within 48 h) is the only proven spike.** Other next-gen explainers have a median of about 45 min.
- **Discovery-dead lanes stay paused (signal).** Impressions stay at 19–62: Medicare (7 min), pharmacy (29), petrelintide (13), elecoglipron (18). The same goes for approved-drug single-trial deep dives (SURMOUNT-5: 32 min; HFpEF: 35) and broad head-term titles.
- **Thumbnails (signal, confounded by impression volume):**
  - Medians: reaction about 6.1% (n=7), before-after about 6.25% (n=2), versus about 3.4% (n=4), big-number about 2.8% (n=7).
  - Reaction didn't rescue Strength (2.6% on 724 impressions), so layout alone isn't the fix.
  - Default: before-after or reaction, 4 words or fewer, one labelled character. No big-number unless it's being tested.
- **Repackaging targets** (most impressions, lowest CTR):
  - What to Eat: 745 impressions, 2.8%, 0.19 min per impression.
  - Strength: 724, 2.6%, 0.38.
  - Change one thing at a time: thumbnail first, then a title that matches the opening.
- **Retention:** median AVD about 5.5 min (35–57%). Open on a "you" scenario that answers the title within 15 s. Re-hook every 45–75 s, and ask for the subscribe once, after the payoff.
- **Ending:** "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description." Never speak a title, and never say "on screen".

## Slots and production
- **Slot effect is small; topic decides.** Shorts at 08:00 / 13:00 / 20:00 ET, long-form at 14:00 ET.
- **Shorts length (57–111 s) doesn't predict views.** Aim for 60–85 s and 170 words or fewer.
- **Carousels reach 0–4 (proven, n≈48).** Stop them.
- **Masking:** mix at 0.02 or lower the first time. It has cost at least 8 pieces.
