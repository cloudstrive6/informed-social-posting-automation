## GLP-1 Shorts: what works (n=14)
- **Proven: a recognizable named drug plus one plain question or one number.**
  - Top six: Mounjaro metabolism (115), tirzepatide fat or muscle (98), semaglutide/tirzepatide and surgery (82), survodutide (63), CagriSema vs tirzepatide (56), online "retatrutide" (55). Their median is about 70, roughly 1.8× the GLP-1 median.
- **Signal: "what does it do to my body?" questions lead.** Metabolism, fat vs muscle and surgery are our top 3 GLP-1 Shorts.
  - Safety-scare topics land below the median even with a calm, hedged answer: pancreatic cancer 23, vision loss (NAION) 32, food noise 32 (n=3).
- **Signal: tirzepatide or Mounjaro in the title beats semaglutide alone.** Median 90 (n=4) vs 32 (n=3). Sample is small, so don't over-fit.
- **Signal: a practical "you" action works.** "Tell your anesthesia team" got 82 views.
- **Underperformers:**
  - generic comparators ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug", 2);
  - feelings or stigma questions ("Is GLP-1 cheating", 6);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab, 21; ACHIEVE-4, 31);
  - teasing a number without giving it (HRT, 5).
- **Title rules:**
  - Use at most one number, in plain words, with its hedge.
  - Use at most one abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
- **Signal: myth-busts about online or "unproven" products work on both platforms.**
  - Online "retatrutide": 55 on YouTube.
  - Plague "cures" online: 79 on YouTube and 23 reach on Instagram.
  - The GLP-1 version warns about gray-market sources. It never names a seller and never says how to buy.
