## Long-form
- **New, high priority: we missed a Lane C slot.** The retatrutide TRANSCEND-T2D-1 Phase 3 result got 223 as a Short, but no long-form was made. When a named next-gen drug posts a new trial result, **publish the long-form first (same day, new drug name first in the title), then the Short as its funnel.** Doing it in that order keeps us within the "no long-form on a topic already Shorted in the last 14 days" ban.
- **Proven-ish (n=6): approved-drug deep dives on a single old trial fail.** Same BMI 1, hair loss 3, SURMOUNT-5 6, pill vs shot 9, HFpEF 14, strength 29. A topic that did well as a Short doesn't carry over (SURMOUNT-5: 162 as a Short, 6 as a long-form).
- **"Did X beat tirzepatide?" (n=3): 475 / 20 / 16.** The win came from the drug being new, not from the frame. Retatrutide long-forms sit at 18–20 whatever the framing.
- **Lane A has n=1 so far: 12 at day 2.**
  - Search traffic builds slowly, so score it at day 7 and day 28 before judging. Plague search-question long-forms got 74, 48 and 16 (median 48).
  - Don't post a same-day Short on the same question: "Tirzepatide Not Working Yet?" (61) went out the day the long-form launched and may have soaked up its demand. Run the spin-off Short the next day, and have it name the long-form.
- **Titles packed with numbers and abbreviations** (for example "T2D+Obesity… Wk 80") don't help. Fresh topics win despite them.
- **Thumbnails:** no signal. Every layout has both hits and flops, and layout is confounded with topic.
