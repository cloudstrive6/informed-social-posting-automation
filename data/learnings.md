## Long-form
1. **Lane C first (signal, n=5).** When a named next-gen drug posts a new trial result or a regulatory step, publish the long-form the same day with the new drug name first in the title. Post the Short at 20:00 as its funnel.
   - We missed this with retatrutide TRANSCEND-T2D-1: the Short got 238 and no long-form was made.
2. **Lane A is promising but unproven (n=2).** "How Long Does Tirzepatide Take to Work?" doubled from day 2 to day 3, the slow search build we expected. Keep making 3 a week and score each one at day 7 and day 28.
   - Plague search-question long-forms reached 77, 48 and 16.
   - The best Lane A titles are a short question people type that names a drug.
3. **Approved-drug deep dives on a single old trial fail (proven-ish, n=6: 1, 3, 7, 10, 16, 33).** A topic that did well as a Short doesn't carry over: SURMOUNT-5 got 163 as a Short and 7 as a long-form.
4. **The "Did X beat tirzepatide?" frame doesn't drive views (n=3: 489, 23, 17).** The drug being new does.
5. **Spin-off Shorts run the day after their long-form and name it.** A same-day Short on the same question ("Not Working Yet?", 62) may compete with the long-form.
6. **Titles:** 60 characters or fewer, at most 1 number, no abbreviations ("T2D", "wk").
7. **Thumbnails:** no signal. Layout is confounded with topic, so don't optimize it yet.
