## Long-form: what earns minutes
- **Suggested video carries Lane A (proven).** RELATED_VIDEO gives 72% of mature Lane A minutes (1,210 of 1,674) at 5–6 min a view (Strength 72/419, What to Eat 70/429, Side Effects 52/261). Search gives 0.2–2.5 min a view.
  - Every new Lane A video should answer a fresh question next to an existing one, so the cluster keeps recommending itself.
- **Subscriber views are worth less than we thought.** Side Effects gets 2.4 min a view, Strength 1.1 and Protein 0.4. Don't judge a video on its first-48 h subscriber spike.
- **PLAYLIST traffic is too small to judge** (n=6 views, 0–12 min each). Keep the playlist, but stop quoting "8–12 min" as proven.
- **Shorts → long funnel is still zero (proven).** No long-form shows a SHORTS or END_SCREEN source.
- **The first spoken sentence must answer the literal title.** Strength's search viewers watch only 0.23 min each because it opens on lean-mass stats.
- **Retention:** Lane A AVD is 5–6.4 min (38–50%). Bones & Muscle holds 73% and Plateau 43%.
  - Open on a "you" scenario after the answer, and re-hook every 45–75 s.
  - One subscribe ask, after the payoff.
  - End with "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description." Never speak a title, and never say "on screen".
- **Thumbnails, compared at 400+ impressions (signal):**
  - Before-after (state change) leads: How Long 4.97%, Protein 3.98%, Side Effects 3.80%.
  - Big-number is mixed: orforglipron 4.16%, What to Eat 2.50% (still falling, from 2.79%), Madagascar 2.1%.
  - Reaction is weak: pill vs shot 2.58%, Strength 2.31%.
  - Default to a state-change design: 4 words or fewer, one labelled character, never a body before/after.
- **What to Eat (1,441 impressions) and Strength (1,341) have a packaging problem, not a discovery problem.** Run Exp E on What to Eat only and keep every other element the same for 14 days. Strength is next.
- **Slow starters are normal for Lane A.** Plateau had 139 impressions at day 6 (4.32% CTR, 36 min). Bones & Muscle had 95 at day 5 (3.16%, 36 min, 73% AVD). Food Noise and Thanksgiving are at days 1–3. Thanksgiving is seasonal, expected to peak Nov 18–27.
- **Banned in long-form:** cost, Medicare, pharmacy and gray-market topics, "What is X?" explainers in the 14:00 slot, single-trial deep dives, and broad head-term titles.
