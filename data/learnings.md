## YouTube Shorts: what works
- **Proven: "tirzepatide" in the title is still our biggest lever.**
  - Titles with it: median 83 (n=11).
  - Titles without it, excluding retatrutide: median 31 (n=9).
- **Signal, our strongest lane: retatrutide and other gray-market or authenticity topics in a calm "what's confirmed?" frame.**
  - Results: 183, 116, 91 and 68. Median 103.5, about 1.9× the median (n=4).
  - Holds even at 20:00 (compounded got 91).
  - Attribute every line to the FDA, MHRA or manufacturer, and date every status.
  - Never name a seller or explain how to buy. Point to a prescriber about approved options or trials.
- **Signal: head-to-heads that name both drugs, generic name first, answered by about 10 s.**
  - Results: SURMOUNT-5 weight 157, stomach side effects 90, CagriSema vs tirzepatide 59, dulaglutide heart failure 53.
  - Median 74.5 (n=4).
  - An unnamed comparator flopped ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug": 2).
- **Signal: muscle works as a question or an outcome, not a how-to.**
  - "Fat or muscle?" got 99. "How to protect your muscle" got 130.
  - "3 Ways to Start Strength Training" got 27.
- **Updated signal: single-drug "Tirzepatide (Brand): does it help [condition]?" Shorts land only near the median on YouTube.**
  - Results: heart failure with preserved ejection fraction 66, diabetic eye disease 46, sleep apnea 42 (median 46, n=3).
  - Use them as backups, not lead Shorts.
  - The brand-in-brackets format no longer stands out on its own: median 99 (n=5), carried by the 2 muscle titles.
- **Signal: "Is [X] approved yet? (Oct 2026)" status titles fail on YouTube** (patch 18, CagriSema 14). Put the dated status inside a named-drug comparison instead.
- **Underperformers (all below median):**
  - Orforglipron Shorts: 36, 31.
  - Stigma: 6.
  - Scare topics about approved drugs: pancreatic cancer 25, vision loss 33.
  - Obscure drugs with crowded numbers: trevogrumab 27.
  - Stopping titles with no drug named: 25.
  - Semaglutide-only titles: 32 and 56.
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first.
  - At most 1 number and 1 abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
  - Crowded titles hold even tirzepatide back.

## Length
- **No link between length and views.** Hits ran 74–97 s; the 83 s CagriSema status Short flopped.
- Aim for 60–85 s at 170 words or fewer. The hard limit is 178 s.
- The 35–50 s test still hasn't run.

## Long-form: the monetization problem
- **Watch time is near zero.** At a median of 11 views per 12-minute video, each long-form earns roughly 1 watch hour. Long-form is not moving us toward the Partner Program yet.
- **Downgraded to an anecdote: "Did [new drug] beat tirzepatide?".** EloraTZP got 416, but the CagriSema long-form in the same frame got 4 (early numbers).
  - EloraTZP's likely edge: it was the first explainer of a brand-new term, with little competition in search.
- **A Short's hit topic doesn't carry to long-form** (SURMOUNT-5: 157 as a Short, 6 as a long-form). Approved-drug long-forms: median 6 (n=6).
- **Working hypothesis to test:** long-form wins when it's the first clear explainer of a newly reported named drug or result. Publish within 24–48 h of the news, with the new name first in the title.
- **Untested:** a long-form in the gray-market "what's confirmed?" lane (retatrutide, counterfeit pens). It's our strongest Short lane.
- **Thumbnails:** no signal.
