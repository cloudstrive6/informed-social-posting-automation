## GLP-1 Shorts on YouTube
- **Proven: "tirzepatide" in the title is the biggest lever we have.** With tirzepatide: median 82 (n=10). Without tirzepatide and not about retatrutide: median 29 (n=9). Semaglutide-only titles: 32.5 (n=4).
- **Signal: drug first, brand in brackets.** "(Mounjaro)" or "Mounjaro…" titles: median 107 (n=4). Use "Tirzepatide (Mounjaro): …?" or "Does Mounjaro …?".
- **Signal, our strongest lane: retatrutide and other gray-market or authenticity topics in a calm "what's confirmed?" frame.** Four Shorts: 183, 116, 81 (compounded vs counterfeit, even at 20:00) and 67. Median 98.5, about 1.8× the median.
  - Retatrutide gets past the emerging-trend penalty (Reddit retatrutide was tagged emerging and has 116 and climbing).
  - Attribute everything to the FDA, MHRA or the manufacturer, and date every status. Never name a seller or say how to buy. Point to a prescriber about approved options or trials.
- **Signal: head-to-heads that name both drugs.**
  - Results: SURMOUNT-5 weight 157, stomach side effects 84, dulaglutide heart failure 51 (day 1), CagriSema vs tirzepatide 59. Median about 72 (n=4).
  - Put the generic name first and give the answer by about 10 s.
  - Unnamed comparators flop ("GLP-1 vs Older Drug" got 2).
- **Signal: evergreen tirzepatide questions hold up** (130, 84, 65, 27; median 74.5, n=4). Evergreen tirzepatide topics are safe as backups.
- **Signal: the muscle lane works only as a question or outcome.** Those got 130 and 99. The numbered how-to "3 Ways to Start Strength Training on Tirzepatide" got 27. Don't repeat that frame.
- **New signal: "Is [X] approved yet? (Oct 2026)" status titles flop on YouTube** (patch 18, CagriSema 7 on day 1). Don't use a bare status question as a main Short. Fold the dated status into a named-drug comparison instead ("Did CagriSema beat tirzepatide?" got 59).
- **Signal: orforglipron Shorts underperform** (34, 29). Keep orforglipron for long-form, or pair it with tirzepatide in the title.
- **Other underperformers:** stigma (6), scare topics about approved drugs (pancreatic cancer 25, NAION 33), obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 27), and "stopping" titles with no drug named (25).
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first, brand in brackets.
  - At most 1 number and 1 abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.
  - Crowded titles hold even tirzepatide back (HFpEF got 65).

  - Muscle plan: 130 YouTube views vs 2 reach. Reddit retatrutide: 116 vs 4. Compounded: 81 vs 4. Patch: 18 vs 85.
  - Don't let one platform overturn a finding from the other.
- **Proven: question titles lead Instagram.** Question titles: median 39 reach (n=14). Statement and how-to titles: 4 (n=7).
- **Updated signal: questions about body effects or safety with both drugs named work on both platforms.** Stomach side effects got 103 reach (84 views) and dulaglutide heart failure got 99 reach (51 views), our two best GLP-1 Reels. A weight-only head-to-head ("Who lost more?") still got 8.
- **Retracted: the dated "approved yet?" question isn't a reliable Instagram winner.** Patch got 85; CagriSema got 0.
- **Gray-market topics reach almost no one on Instagram** (4, 4). For Instagram, rely on the body-effect questions instead.
