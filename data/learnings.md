## Long-form craft
- **Shorts → long funnel: still zero (proven, 24 long-forms).** SHORTS and END_SCREEN traffic is 0 everywhere, and PLAYLIST has about 9 views in total.
  - Links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable. Only the owner's Studio related-video link works.
  - End screens, a line-1 ▶ Watch next link and the "Life on Tirzepatide" playlist are the cheapest unused lever.
- **Structure:** the first sentence answers the literal title, then a "you" scenario. Re-hook every 45–75 s. One subscribe ask, right after the first payoff. End with "There's a video I'd watch next, linked in the description."
- **Retention (signal, n=6):** the best AVD comes from cuts of about 10 min: Bones 62% (9:54), Constipation 54% (11:24) and Stopping 52% (10:40). Protein (31%) and Side Effects (37%) are the weakest. Aim for 9–11 min.
- **Thumbnails (signal, n=4 at 1,000+ impressions):**
  - State change: Side Effects 4.13% and Protein 3.61%.
  - Big number: What to Eat 2.68%.
  - Reaction: Strength 2.03%.
  - Below about 300 impressions, CTR doesn't separate layouts. Stopping hit 8.84% with a reaction thumbnail, so topic demand matters at least as much.
  - Default to a state change: 4 words or fewer, one labelled character, never a body or weight before/after.
- **Exp E:** What to Eat's CTR went from 2.46% to 2.68% after the swap. That's too early to call; read it on 11-24.
- **Banned in long-form:** cost, Medicare, pharmacy and gray-market topics (Medicare 30 min, pharmacy 63); "What is X?" pipeline explainers; single-trial deep dives (SURMOUNT-5 got 36); broad head terms.

- **Title rules:** "Is this normal?" or "Will it…?" first, with tirzepatide first in the title. Semaglutide-only titles get about 40 views.
- **Head-to-heads:** tirzepatide-first ones in an obesity population get 108–176. Diabetes-only ones get 31–60.

- **Underperformers:** observational, survey or grocery angles (8–9), surgery (8), cost and Medicare (42), "3 Ways" lists (38), drug names nobody searches for (28–39), add-on-indication trials (PsA 28).
