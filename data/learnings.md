## Long-form retention (d7 curves, n=18, long-form only)
- **Median hold at 30 s is about 77%.** Best: Constipation 91.7, Petrelintide 89.5, Plateau 83.3, Bones 82.8. Worst: Strength 41.4 (the claim didn't match the title) and Protein 51.8.
- **The biggest drop comes at 12–35 s in 13 of 18 curves (proven),** right after the first answer, where the open loop or roadmap starts.
  - **Fix:** at 15–30 s, give a second concrete payoff (one number or the first step). Keep the open loop to one sentence. No roadmap there.
- **First chapter change at 70–75 s (n=2: Stopping, Constipation).**
  - **Fix:** land a payoff before chapter 1 ends, and open chapter 2 with a question.
- **Late drop at 73–85% (n=3: How Long, Plateau, Bones),** where the recap starts.
  - **Fix:** land the last payoff first and start the end-screen ask at about 85%.
- **"You" scenario openers hold best (signal, n≈9):** s30 of 77–92%. News or stakes openers hold 63–72%.
- **The midpoint is weak (proven).** Median `relative_pct_mid` is about 43, and 13 of 18 curves are below 50.
  - **Fix:** re-hook every 45–75 s.
- **Length:** aim for 9–11 min.
