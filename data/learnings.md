## GLP-1 Shorts: what works on YouTube (n=20)
- **Proven: a named drug people recognise, plus one plain question, action or number.**
  - Top 8: bootleg "Reta" 165, tirzepatide muscle plan 130, Mounjaro metabolism 115, SURMOUNT-5 tirzepatide vs semaglutide 109 (day 1), tirzepatide fat or muscle 98, anesthesia 82, survodutide 64, online retatrutide 58.
  - Every one of them is 1.0–2.8× the GLP-1 median.
- **Close to proven: tirzepatide or Mounjaro in the title.**
  - With tirzepatide/Mounjaro: median 98 (n=7: 130, 115, 109, 98, 82, 56, 55).
  - Semaglutide alone: median 32 (n=4). The best of that group is the pill at 55.
- **Signal: muscle on tirzepatide is our best evergreen lane** (n=2: 130 and 98).
  - The winning hook: a direct "you" line, a named drug and a numbered promise ("Losing weight on tirzepatide? 3 ways to protect your muscle"), answered with data by about 15 s.
- **New signal: head-to-heads that name both drugs work.** "Tirzepatide vs Semaglutide: Who Lost More Weight?" got 109 in 1 day. Compare "GLP-1 vs Older Drug", which got 2. Name both sides and give the answer by about 10 s.
- **Signal: warnings about gray-market or "research" products work** (n=3: bootleg Reta 165, plague "cures online" 83, online retatrutide 58).
  - Keep the tone calm and attributed: FDA reports and the manufacturer's statements, plus "what's confirmed vs not".
  - Never name a seller or say how to buy. Always point to a prescriber.
- **Signal: a "what's confirmed?" decoder works when a named drug is in the news** (Reta 165, plague Russia 88). News fades within days, so don't build a lane on one event.
- **Signal: crowded titles hold back even tirzepatide.** "Tirzepatide HFpEF: Worse HF or CV Death 9.9% vs 15.3% Placebo" got 55, the lowest tirzepatide Short. It had 3 abbreviations and 2 numbers. The same idea as a plain question ("Can tirzepatide help one type of heart failure?") fits the rules below better.
- **Signal: scare topics about approved drugs land low**, even when the script is calm (pancreatic cancer 25, NAION 32). Warnings about unapproved products are different and do well.
- **Signal: "stopping a GLP-1" is weak on YouTube** (n=2: 25 and 2). Neither title named a drug. If we try again, name the drug and trial in the title (for example tirzepatide, SURMOUNT-4).
- **Underperformers:**
  - generic or unnamed comparators (2, 25);
  - stigma or feelings questions ("cheating", 6);
  - obscure drugs with crowded numbers (trevogrumab 25, ACHIEVE-4 33);
  - teasing a number without giving it (HRT, 5).
- **Title rules:**
  - Name the drug, generic first.
  - At most one number, with its hedge.
  - At most one abbreviation.
  - Name both sides of every comparison.
  - Answer the title's question within 10–15 s.

## Length
- **Weak signal.** Our 4 best GLP-1 Shorts all ran 74–79 s (Reta, muscle plan, metabolism, SURMOUNT-5).
- We have no proven link between length and views (57–111 s across all Shorts).
- Aim for about 60–80 s at 170 words or fewer. The hard limit is 178 s. The 35–50 s test still hasn't run.

## Principles (untested by our data; keep them)
- The title and the first spoken line share one hook.
- Positive, encouraging GLP-1 tone. Mention common side effects briefly and helpfully. Never fear-bait.
- Lead with the generic name and put the brand in brackets, for recognition only.
