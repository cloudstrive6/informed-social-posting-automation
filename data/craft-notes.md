_Based on 16 QA records from 2026-09-26 to 2026-09-28: 4 carousels, 6 Shorts and 6 long-form videos. 6 were held. On 09-28 only 1 of 8 was held: S1, dropped for an "and" vs "or" scope error. Long-form visuals have not improved. They scored 5.6–5.9, with 24–49 weak shots per video. Across the 6 long-form videos the layout audit found 114 sparse frames, 77 with off-screen text and 46 with tiny text. Shorts improved to 6.2–7.4, but small labels colliding with captions or headlines is now their main problem (35 overlap findings in 3 Shorts). All 6 long-form YouTube descriptions were cut off._

### Motion Designer
- **Measure the box's final bottom edge, including the pill's height and the end of its animation, and keep it at or above 92% of the frame height.** That means y ≤ 994 on a 1080-tall frame and y ≤ 1766 on a 1920-tall frame. Keep a 5% side margin, and wrap or shrink long title pills. Label pills under characters, chip rows and bottom captions keep getting clipped: "BMI 26", Before/During/After at y≈1159, "Calories" at y≈1928, and a title pill running past the right edge (77 off-screen findings in long-form, plus S1, S2 and S3).
- **Never open or hold a shot on a lone icon or cell on a plain gradient.** Every sampled frame, including the first one, needs a headline and hero art or a scene backdrop covering at least about 25% of the frame. Room scenes are fine, but scale the mascot up. This is still the most common finding: 114 sparse findings across 6 long-form videos, often at 4–12% coverage.
- **In Shorts, keep the caption band and the headline box off-limits.** Put small tags ("Pancreas", "Brown fat", "Lowest dose", "60 weeks · T2D") only in a clear zone at least 40px from both. Remove the previous shot's stat pill before new pills enter. This caused 35 overlap findings in S1, S2 and S3, most of them tag-on-caption.
- **Keep the headline and the mascot or hero face in separate zones.** Put the headline in the top band and the mascot below it, and make sure no text crosses the eyes. Stat pills don't go on the hero art either. Seen in about 8 L1/L2/L3 notes, plus 6 on-hero findings in S1 and S3.
- **Keep charts inside their own box, below the big number, the headline and the qualifier line.** Bars cut through text in 6 notes across 3 long-form videos, for example "e Country, o Answers", the "4" in "12.8–14.4%" and "Diagnosed cases per 1,000".
- **Keep decorations, icons and speech bubbles off text.** This covers question marks, DNA shapes, pill and syringe icons, person icons and room props. If one can't be moved, put the text on a solid panel. About 11 notes, for example "How ?mmon Is It?" and a pill icon over "Medicine".
- **Use text of at least about 40px on a 1080 frame, or drop the label.** Labels around 23–24px were flagged every time (46 tiny-text findings in long-form, plus S1 and S3).

### Scriptwriters
- **Write narration text in normal spelling, never phonetic respellings.** Write "Wegovy", "Mounjaro", "CagriSema", "semaglutide", "GLP-1", "FDA", not "wee go vee", "monjaro", "kegri", "sem uh gloo" or "fd a". The QA compares the audio against your literal text, so respellings break both the narration and subtitle checks (L1, 7 scenes in L2, S2, S3). Also use American spelling ("recognized"), write numbers the voice might misread as words, and write acronyms solid ("CRP", not "C R P"). Earlier narration mismatches caused 2 holds.
- **On-screen text can't be stronger than the narration.** It gets fact-checked on its own. For example, "a guess", a bare "<5%" and a flat "not yet published" (3 findings).
- **Include the key limitations named by the authors or independent experts, with the population they apply to.** For example, "only drinks stayed significant" or "all participants were premenopausal". Put the advice with the strongest evidence first. Leaving this out caused the L1 hold on 09-27, and it came up again in S3.
- **Match certainty to the statistics, and compare like with like.** Don't present p=0.05 as being as solid as p=0.02. Label relative risk as relative and give the absolute numbers next to it. Don't put raw drops next to placebo-adjusted differences, or figures with different denominators, without a note (4 findings: L2, L3, C2).
- **Our audience is in the US, UK, CA and AU, so give lab values in both mg/dL and mmol/L** (2 findings in L1).

### Carousel Designer
- **Stat, range and chart slides need a qualifier line under the big number** saying what it counts: self-reported, modelled, two separate cohorts (44% / 48%), or a subgroup. The same applies to Short stat pills and thumbnail numbers, for example "−49% risk" (6 findings: C1, C2, S1, L1).
- **Put a source tag on every slide that makes a claim, and make the title promise only what the slides deliver.** C1 cited sources that no slide linked to, and its title promised a "5-part checklist" the slides didn't deliver (3 findings).

### Social Copywriter & packaging
- **YouTube descriptions: keep them to 3,500 characters or fewer, and put the full sources block before the chapters.** Use this order: a quick answer of 3 lines or less, then the disclaimer and prescriber line, then 6 sources or fewer (short title + URL), then 8 chapters or fewer. If it runs long, cut "What you'll learn" and chapters, never sources. Check that the last line is complete. All 6 long-form descriptions were cut off mid-word or mid-URL, and each time it was a major finding.
- **Fill every packaging field for every item:** YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Threads captions, plus the thumbnail or cover text. Long-form packages shipped YouTube-only 4 times (major each time). C2 left the YouTube fields blank, and S1 and S2 had no thumbnail text.
- **End every caption with the exact line: "It's a prescription medicine. Talk to your doctor or prescriber about whether it's right for you."** It was missing or reworded in C2 Facebook and Threads, S1 Instagram and Facebook, and S2 (5 findings).
- **Before writing, copy the key facts from the verified summary: sample, subgroup vs whole cohort, "and" vs "or", exact outcome and effect size.** Use them identically everywhere. A scope mismatch means REVISE or a hold (C1 and S1 on 09-26, and S1 on 09-28, where the whole 313,145 cohort was described as BMI 25–26.9).
- **Don't make it stronger or wider than the script.** Keep "on average", "seemed tied", "estimated", "what trials show" (not "proven" or "well-supported"), and "often grows back" (not "yet"). Keep who an approval covers. About 13 findings across C1, C2, S1, S3, L1, L2 and L3.
- **Use generic names first. If you use a brand, use the one that matches the trial population**, for example Mounjaro for a type 2 diabetes trial and Wegovy for a Wegovy trial, and don't lead with Ozempic. Avoid weight-loss hashtags and emojis that undercut the message (#WeightLossJourney on a muscle post, 🔥 on a post correcting "fat burning") (4 findings).

### Everyone
- **Name the exact population.** SELECT = adults with established cardiovascular disease (prior heart attack, stroke or symptomatic peripheral artery disease) and overweight or obesity, not "heart disease". Say "coronary heart disease" if that's the exclusion. Keep "both markers" apart from "either marker". Use the analysed sample size (64,743, not 103,684; 53, not 54). An "or both" error caused the S1 hold, and this came up in 7 findings overall.
- **Say exactly what was measured.** Common swaps to avoid: prevalence → "new cases", activity calories → "daily calories", hit rate → "memory", appetite → "food noise", a non-inferiority goal → "main goal" without saying what it was, a modelled figure → a measured one. Don't paraphrase guideline wording into something stronger, and don't put paraphrases in quote marks (about 16 findings).
- **Every claim needs a cited source that actually says it.** If it doesn't, cite the source that does (for example ATTAIN-2 in The Lancet, not the ATTAIN-1 NEJM paper, or FDA's biotin guidance, not the troponin page), or cut the line (about 9 findings: C1, C2, S3, L2 ×2, L3).
- **Hedge absolute and time-sensitive claims.** Write "few studies have…" or "no full paper yet as of [month]". Check event dates against today, since EASD 2026 runs 28 Sept–2 Oct (3 findings: S3 ×2, L3).
- **Source list hygiene.** Use the exact published or registry title, a URL that isn't region-blocked (www.cnn.com, not edition.cnn.com), the page's current date, and the journal issue year. When the date matters, keep "carried out" and "published" apart (7 findings).
