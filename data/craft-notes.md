### Motion Designer
- **Long-forms: check beat sync on the render, not the plan.** Re-time every cut from the final word timestamps, after the final narration and again after any scene is regenerated. Then measure every beat in the rendered file and fix any cut more than 0.5 s off. Check that no shot shows another scene's text: one such shot gets the video dropped even at 91% on time. Plans were 96–100% on time; renders were 76–91%. (5 holds. 2 of them were wrong-scene shots: L1 11-09 and L2 11-16.)
- **Measure masking last, on the final file, and never hand off anything above 0.02.** Duck the music under all narration. Never put a stinger, whoosh or sound effect on a spoken number, drug name or safety line. Re-measure after every re-render; the gate let 0.022 and 0.028 through. (10 pieces over 0.02, latest S1 11-16 at 0.031. Part of 4 holds.)
- **Check that every scene has voice right to the end, then compare a speech-to-text transcript with the script word for word.** Check every "point", number, unit, drug name and key how-to phrase, and regenerate any scene that fails before you mix. (4 holds: silent closing scenes S2 11-12, "the gray plunger is visible" missing S1 11-16, "of the day" L1 11-11. Also "point" dropped L1 11-13 and "tears of petide" L1 11-04.)
- **Build captions from the script's on-screen text field, never from the narration.** Numbers are digits that match the stat pill exactly ("14.8%", "+14%", "25–29%", "<6%", "72"). Before render, search the captions for POINT, PERCENT, HUNDRED, THOUSAND, spelled-out numbers and pronunciation spellings ("NAY-ON", "SULFONYL UREA"). (About 20 Shorts; subtitle checks flagged this 4 times, latest S2 11-15.)
- **Each caption card is one whole clause on 2 lines or fewer.** Never split a word, number or phrase across cards ("SIDE / EFFECTS", "TYPE / TWO DIABETES", "SEVENTY- / TWO"). Never end a card on a comma, dash, colon, THE, A, ABOUT, MAY, WON'T, DOESN'T, BEEN, TYPE, "AND IN" or a bare number. From content date 2026-11-17, the last card is the task's last line, as one whole sentence ending in a full stop. (Nearly every Short, latest all 3 Shorts on 11-16.)
- **Shorts tags: clear the previous card, tags and bubbles before the next card appears.** Keep tags above y 1150, show at most 2 at once, and never place one behind the caption pill. Drop any tag that repeats the caption, and render every chip the shot list asks for. (About 215 overlap findings across 23 Shorts, latest S3 11-16 with 12.)
- **Long-form tags: the bottom of every tag stays at y 1000 or less, and every label is at least ~40px.** Name, group and dose tags keep clipping past y 1080 ("Placebo group", "15 mg arm", "≥20% lost", "Nausea", "Eli Lilly"), and labels keep rendering at 22–26px. (About 215 off-screen and 255 tiny-text findings, latest L3 11-16 with 30 off-screen.)
- **Never use these backdrops:** the rounded teal or green "cabinet strip", the hanging grey or orange ornament circles, or a starfield on a body beat. Match the backdrop to the beat's mood: night → night room, brand → teal gradient, clinic → clinic, body → body backdrop. (Cabinet strips in 14 pieces since 11-10, latest L1, L2, L3 and S2 on 11-16. Starfield on a body beat in about 11 pieces, latest S1, L2 and L3 on 11-16. Night beats in a daytime room in 4 pieces.)
- **Draw people as whole characters with a neck and shoulders, and keep each named character looking the same throughout.** No rings, donuts or blobs as the main character, and no mascots perched on a person. On risk and caveat lines, use a calm, neutral mascot: no smiles, no green checks, no sweating. (Floating heads in about 15 pieces, latest L1, L2, L3 and S1 on 11-16. Rings in about 7.)
- **Fill every frame with a headline plus a labelled hero covering at least ~25% of it, starting with the opening frame.** In the first 30 s of a long-form, the main character fills about a third of the frame height. Keep stat titles off the hero art. Document and doctor beats on a plain gradient are the usual empty ones: put the stat on the document and add a character. No blank frames, empty bubbles, cropped shapes or stray debug icons. (About 145 near-empty findings in long-forms. Tiny opening character in L2 and L3 11-16, stray icon L1 11-16.)

### Scriptwriters
- **Shorts: stay inside the task's length arm and never go over 160 spoken words. Count every safety and closing line yourself.** Tight arm: 90–120 words. Standard arm: 135–160. From content date 2026-11-17, the ending is "Not personal advice, so ask your prescriber.", then the emergency and don't-stop lines when the topic needs them, then the task's last line (a loop back into sentence 1). Never write a subscribe line or "link in the description" in a Short.
- **Copy the fixed safety lines exactly, and speak each one and show it on screen.** An emergency line names its trigger and keeps "right away". Follow it with "For anything that isn't one of those emergency signs, don't stop or change your medicine on your own." Never write "Otherwise". Unapproved drugs or uses "aren't approved". (About 12 pieces.)
- **Write narration exactly as it should be spoken, and keep it evergreen.** Give each decimal its own short sentence, with the unit spoken. Say "loss" or "lower" for minus figures (L1 11-16). Never write "now", "this week", "new" or dates in narration, thumbnails or on-screen text (S2 11-04, L2 11-11, L3 11-16). Help lines cover all four markets. For chest pain or fainting, the emergency number comes first.
- **Keep each finding at its source's exact unit, scope, verb and hedge, including in recaps.** Each number names its measure, drug and arm, comparator, population and timeframe in the same sentence. A share of people is never an "average". Never present slower stomach emptying as the cause of an effect (no "so", no "that's why"; 5 pieces). Don't compare two different trials as if they were head-to-head. Never add "most", "many", "commonly", "similar", "purely" or a timeframe the source doesn't give ("a few days", L3 11-16). Never write "the paper says" unless you've read the sentence in it. (About 100 findings. Recaps lost scope in L3 11-14 and L2 11-16.)
- **Run the muted-viewer test, starting with the opening card.** The opening card names the drug and the scope from sentence 1 (S1 11-12, S3 11-13, S1 11-15, S1 11-16, L3 11-16). Every stat card names the drug, dose, both groups, population and timeframe. On-screen text is never stronger or narrower than the narration. (About 70 findings.)

### Carousel Designer
- **Carousels are stopped. If the scheduler still asks for one, reuse verified Short text only.** Use each trial's exact population and a stat card that names the drug, both groups and the measure. Use generic drug hashtags only, give every source its full revised date, use one save ask, and end on the exact disclaimer plus the prescriber line. (All 3 carousels had findings.)

### Social Copywriter & packaging
- **Long-form descriptions must be complete and 1,250 characters or fewer.** Build the safety block first, with the scoped don't-stop line and the closing disclaimer. Then add a quick answer, then 2–3 sources. Check that the field ends "…whether it's right for you." Never type a placeholder ("▶ Watch next", "MM:SS"). Add chapters only with real timestamps starting at 00:00, one per section including the final checklist; otherwise leave them out and say so in the post notes. (5 cut off or missing the ending, 1 of them a hold. Over the limit in 3, latest L1 11-16. Checklist chapter missing in 2.)
- **Line 1 stays empty on every YouTube description, Shorts included, and the field holds viewer copy only, never an internal note.** (Line 1 filled in about 8 pieces, latest S2 11-15. Internal note in L1 11-06.)
- **Every long-form narration promises a watch-next link, so the post notes must name the target video from the playbook's linking list.** New broad videos → Side Effects; First Month → Months 2–6. The publisher adds that verified video URL. Never use the channel home page, and never leave the link out. (3 in one day: none in L1 and L3 11-16, the L3 one a packaging REVISE; channel home page in L2 11-16.)
- **Every caption field ends on exactly "Not personal advice. Talk to your doctor or prescriber about whether it's right for you." Put the hashtags and the don't-stop line before it.** Copy safety lines word for word: keep "right away", "healthcare provider" and "with or without vomiting". Write "medicines", never "meds", "Otherwise" or "won't let up". Threads stays at about 300 characters: cut facts, never a safety line. If the safety lines alone run past 300, ship the safety lines plus one fact. (Threads over the limit in about 35 pieces, latest all 3 Shorts on 11-16.)
- **Copy facts, qualifiers and populations word for word from the verified script, on every platform and in the pinned comment.** Keep "top dose", "all doses combined", "Phase 2b" and the full trial population. The pinned comment never cuts the population to "adults" (L1 11-04, L1 11-15, L2 11-16). Never credit our own tips to a study (S3 11-05, S3 11-15). Never add hooks the script doesn't support ("You're not imagining it", "Not what it looks like", "yet"). (About 135 findings, in every batch.)
- **Titles, thumbnails, first lines and hashtags keep the video's hedge and scope.** A stat in a title names its measure. Don't promise a timeframe the video doesn't give. Use one save or share ask, never an open question. Hashtags are only the generic names of drugs whose own data the post reports. Banned examples: #Zepbound, #GLP1 on a single-drug post, #PatientSafety, #Type2Diabetes, #WeightManagement, #sideeffects, #pentips, #HeartRate, #MetabolicHealth. (About 52 pieces, latest L1 and S1 11-16.)

### Everyone
- **The Medication Guide and the IFU each need their own working URL, never the PI page or a PI anchor.** Open the URL and find the quoted sentence before you cite it. If it won't load, paraphrase without quotes and tag the PI section instead. If the keyword names Mounjaro, cite Mounjaro's own IFU. (Caused the L2 11-14 failure; 9 pieces.)
- **Give every result its exact drug scope and trial population.**
  - **Obesity trials:** SURMOUNT-1, -4 and -5, STEP 1 and ATTAIN-1 enrolled adults with obesity, or overweight plus a weight-related condition, without diabetes. Never shorten this to "no diabetes" or "adults".
  - **Zepbound pooled data:** Study 1 excluded type 2 diabetes; Study 2 enrolled only people with it.
  - **Pancreatitis:** show 0.23 vs 0.11 per 100 years (diabetes pool) only alongside the weight-trial figure, 0.14 vs 0.15.
  - **GI effects:** they came "mostly during dose escalation and decreased over time".
  - **75/25 fat-to-lean split:** this is from SURMOUNT-1's 160-person DXA substudy, not the whole trial.
  - **SURMOUNT-4:** 335 vs 335; +14.0% average regain on placebo, and placebo still ended 9.9% below baseline.
  - **Hair loss:** 5.4% vs 0.9% (women 7.1%, men 0.5%).
  - **Injection-site reactions:** 6–8% by dose vs about 2% on placebo.
  - **Foundayo:** the label's top dose is 17.2 mg, with −11.1% in Trial 1. The 36 mg dose and −11.2% are from the trial.
- **Tag a line to a source only after you find the sentence that says it.** Our own tips, help lines and safety lines get no tag, and a source title lists only the sections it is actually cited for. Known facts: the July 2023 review was EMA PRAC's; name both NANOS and the AAO; NHS "more at risk" is not the same as "more likely"; the removed label warning was "Suicidal Behavior and Ideation". (About 65 findings.)
- **Keep the source list clean: fetch every URL first.** Reject 402, 403 and 404 responses, login and DOI redirects, newsroom pages and search-query pages, and use the PubMed record for paywalled journals. Every entry carries its live revised or reviewed date, never just a year. Known dates: Zepbound PI 28 Aug 2026; Mounjaro PI 27 Aug 2026; NHS diarrhoea pages 01 May 2026 and 21 Dec 2023.
